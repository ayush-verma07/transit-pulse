    About this project

    Transit Pulse polls the MTA's live GTFS-Realtime subway feed every 15 seconds and reconciles each vehicle update against the agency's published static schedule to work out how early or late trains actually are running, stop by stop and route by route. This page is a live view into that data: an approximate map of where trains currently are, and a lookup tool for a stop's recent on-time performance.

    Motivation

    I built this after a week alone in New York City, where I got completely lost more times than I'd like to admit. I would stand on a platform, staring at my phone, having no idea if the train I needed was two minutes away or twenty. NYC's transit system is massive and mostly a black box to riders: you get a scheduled time, but no sense of whether that time means anything. This project exists because I wanted an honest answer to a simple question: is my NYC train actually coming when it says it will?
